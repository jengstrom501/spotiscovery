Import full history

On your Spotify privacy page, request Account data (your last year, within about 5 days) or Extended streaming history (everything, up to 30 days). Upload the zip Spotify sends you, or unzip it yourself and upload the StreamingHistory_music_*.json / Streaming_History_Audio_*.json files.

Not music

Podcasts, audiobooks and sleep sounds found in your history. Hidden ones are left out of every chart and pick. Hide anything else from the Top artists list on Trends.
